# Milestone 10: what the slice still lacked

Written 2026-10-23, after six weeks idle. The bar is §44 of `BROWSER_AAA_ENGINE_PROJECT_KICKOFF.md` (one complete mission of the Task Unit sequel: three objectives, 8 to 12 enemies with routes and level alert states, extraction, 5 to 10 minutes, showing movement, combat, enemy AI, interaction, environment, dynamic lighting, particles, animation, post and audio) and `docs/design/mission-shape.md` (Insert → three objectives → Extract, a checkpoint at each, Reach / Plant / Eliminate as level data, the stealth-to-combat alert model).

## Where it stood on 2026-09-07

The street ran: reach the square, set the charge on the depot wall, return to the insert. Eight patrolling riflemen on a baked navmesh, the per-enemy awareness ladder, the Quiet → Alerted → Lockdown sector model with reinforcements from authored ingress points, hitscan gunplay with gore and ragdolls, 113 generated audio takes, checkpoint reloads. 415 tests, seven gameplay probes.

## The gaps, and what closed them

| # | Gap | Against | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Only two objectives.** The third "objective" was the walk back to the insert, so the mission was Reach → Plant → Extract, one short of three objectives plus extraction. | §44, mission shape | **closed**: Reach the square → Eliminate the broker → Set the charge → Extract. Extract is its own objective kind (a Reach that ends the run). |
| 2 | **Eliminate was never used.** The runner had the kind, but it meant "every hostile dead", not the shape's "kill a tagged entity". No level carried one. | mission shape | **closed**: `target` on an Eliminate names a patrol route; the street authors the broker (`role=hvt`: pale coat, gold trim that glows, 160 health) on a beat inside two riflemen's loops. The marker and the distance chip follow him. |
| 3 | **Plant had no defend half.** The shape's Plant is "hold interact at a site, then defend a timer". Holding F finished the objective on the spot: no fuse, no defence, no detonation. | mission shape | **closed**: the hold arms a 20 s fuse (`fuse` in level data). Arming trips the depot alarm (`AlertDirector.raise` → alerted, hunt on the charge) and the sector sends two hostiles to kneel at it (`Enemy.assignWork`); four seconds of work pulls it and the objective is back to the hold. A sighting of the operator outranks the job. |
| 4 | **Nothing happened at extraction.** Reaching the insert showed a "mission complete" band and the street carried on: the operator could still be shot, and dying afterwards showed the failed and complete bands together. No debrief, no way to play again short of reloading the page. | §44 ("complete mission") | **closed**: extraction ends the run on a debrief (time, hostiles down, accuracy, times spotted, the sector's peak tier, checkpoint reloads, a rating), the street stands down (hostiles see an operator who is no longer there), and Enter plays it again from the insert with the whole garrison back. |
| 5 | **No insert.** The mission opened on the click-to-play controls card. Nothing said what the operation was or what the objectives were. | mission shape (Insert) | **closed**: the card carries the operation's name and the four steps, struck through as they are done, so pausing doubles as the objective list. |
| 6 | **HUD objective icon froze on the first one.** `hud.ts` replaced the icon with `outerHTML` and kept the detached node, so every later objective kept the flag icon. | bug | **closed**: the icon swaps inside a slot that keeps its place. |
| 7 | **No set piece for dynamic lighting and particles in the mission itself.** Neon, muzzle flashes, rain and steam were all ambient; nothing the player does lights or fills the street. | §44 (dynamic lighting, particles) | **closed**: `combat/demolition.ts`. The charge's fuse light blinks faster as it burns; the blast is a 5,200 cd flash that lights the depot end, a fireball, 520 sparks, debris, a smoke plume, then a burning wreck (glow light, smoke column, embers) for the rest of the run, a scorch decal, damage by distance and line of sight, and a camera shake (`ShoulderCamera.shake`, new in the engine). Six new cues, plus the lockdown stinger that had never been generated. |
| 8 | **No probe plays the mission through.** The suite proved each mechanic alone; nothing proved Insert → Extract completes. | CLAUDE.md probe rule | **closed**: `start-to-extraction` walks and shoots the whole mission headless and checks the debrief and the replay; `charge-is-defended` proves the defuse. `--shots` saves the beats. |
| 9 | **Perf baseline red.** Every entry read high on `cpuMs` against a baseline recorded on a quiet machine, and the mission's row predated the draw-call work (1053 draws then, 721 since). | perf suite | **closed**: on a quiet GPU on 2026-10-23 the unchanged baseline read green across all nine entries, so September's red was contention. The mission's row got its exact counters updated (718 draws, from a stale 1053 that would have let a 330-draw regression pass) and kept its quiet September timings; the Milestone 10 build was proven no slower than HEAD by A/B under the same load. Along the way the particle system learned to skip idle emitters, which paid for the demolition rig's own draws. Reasons and numbers in `tools/benchmarks/README.md`. |
| 10 | **One lane per objective.** The street is a single 110 m corridor with sidewalks; the shape asks for two or three approaches per objective and verticality through mantle zones and ladders. | mission shape (level structure) | open: a level-design pass in Blender (side alleys, a mantle route, a rooftop) and a new navmesh bake. The next milestone's work, not a fix. |
| 11 | **Length unproven.** Nobody has timed a real playthrough against the 5 to 10 minute target. | §44 | open, needs Greg. The scripted run (no stealth, no deaths, rounds switched off) is the floor; a real run is a playtest. |
| 12 | **Nobody ever lost contact** (found while building #3). Once a hostile went alert his awareness pinned at 1 and the sight check re-ran `becomeAlert` every tick, which refreshed the contact clock, so Alert → Searching → Unaware never happened while the operator lived and the sector could never settle back down in real play. Probes only ever broke contact by killing the operator. | mission shape (alert model) | **closed**: alert is entered on the transition only, and losing contact drops awareness below the alert line. `charge-is-defended` asserts the descent (the workers lose an operator who has gone, then go to the charge). |
| 13 | **The objective marker never hid or took its radius** (found in the captures). `RenderSync` writes visibility and scale from the entity every frame, so the runner's `marker.visible` and `marker.scale` were overwritten: every ring was 1 m and the ring stayed up behind the end of the mission. | bug | **closed**: both go through the `Renderable` and `Transform` components; a unit test holds it. |
